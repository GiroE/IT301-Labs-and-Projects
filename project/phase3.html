Phase Three: The Infinity Saga Concludes

Phase Three takes place from 2016 to 2019. This phase introduces new heroes, challenges the Avengers, and brings the Infinity Saga to its conclusion.

2016: Divided Heroes and New Beginnings

Captain America: Civil War

The Avengers become divided over government oversight, leading to conflict between Captain America and Iron Man.

Doctor Strange

Stephen Strange discovers the mystical arts and becomes a powerful sorcerer.

2017: New Heroes and Adventures

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2

The Guardians continue their adventures while Peter Quill learns more about his family.

Spider-Man: Homecoming

Peter Parker balances his everyday life with his responsibilities as Spider-Man.

Thor: Ragnarok

Thor must escape an unfamiliar planet and attempt to save Asgard from destruction.

2018: The Infinity War Begins

Black Panther

T'Challa becomes king of Wakanda and faces challenges to his leadership.

Avengers: Infinity War

The Avengers and their allies unite to stop Thanos from collecting the Infinity Stones.

Ant-Man and the Wasp

Scott Lang teams up with Hope van Dyne to explore the mysteries of the Quantum Realm.

2019: The End of an Era

Captain Marvel

Carol Danvers discovers her origins and becomes one of the MCU's most powerful heroes.

Avengers: Endgame

The surviving Avengers attempt to reverse the destruction caused by Thanos.

Spider-Man: Far From Home

Peter Parker faces new challenges while dealing with the aftermath of Endgame.

Why Phase Three Matters

Phase Three brings together the characters and storylines introduced throughout the MCU. It concludes the Infinity Saga while setting up future Marvel adventures.
