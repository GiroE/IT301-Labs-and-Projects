Phase One: The Avengers Assemble

Phase One (2008–2012) introduces the original Avengers and establishes the Marvel Cinematic Universe. These six movies build toward the formation of Earth's Mightiest Heroes.

Phase One Movies

Iron Man (2008)

Tony Stark becomes Iron Man after developing a powerful armored suit.

The Incredible Hulk (2008)

Bruce Banner struggles to control his transformation into the Hulk.

Iron Man 2 (2010)

Tony Stark faces new enemies while dealing with the consequences of revealing his identity.

Thor (2011)

Thor is banished from Asgard and must learn what it means to become a worthy hero.

Captain America: The First Avenger (2011)

Steve Rogers becomes Captain America and fights HYDRA during World War II.

The Avengers (2012)

Earth's Mightiest Heroes join forces to stop Loki and his invasion of Earth.

Why Phase One Matters

Phase One establishes the main heroes, introduces S.H.I.E.L.D., and sets up the larger story of the Infinity Saga.
